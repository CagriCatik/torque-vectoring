# Telemetry Schema, Diagnostic Plots, and Animation Reference

This document specifies the exported CSV telemetry columns, diagnostic report sections, and animation views generated by [`src/torquevector/visualization/`](../src/torquevector/visualization/) and [`src/torquevector/infrastructure/telemetry_io.py`](../src/torquevector/infrastructure/telemetry_io.py).

---

## 1 Exported CSV Telemetry Columns (`telemetry/<controller>.csv`)

Every simulation run writes a high-resolution time-series CSV file per controller verified for exact numerical consistency with internal solver states (`Q12.4`):

| Column Group | Columns | Units | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Time & Global Pose** | `t_s`, `x_m`, `y_m`, `yaw_rad` | $\text{s}, \text{m}, \text{m}, \text{rad}$ | Simulation timestamp and inertial CG pose $(X, Y, \psi)$ |
| **Chassis Velocities & Slip** | `vx_ms`, `vy_ms`, `yaw_rate_rads`, `beta_rad` | $\text{m/s}, \text{m/s}, \text{rad/s}, \text{rad}$ | Body-frame velocities $(v_x, v_y)$, yaw rate $r$, and sideslip $\beta = \operatorname{atan2}(v_y, v_x)$ |
| **Chassis Accelerations & Net Loads** | `ax_ms2`, `ay_ms2`, `fx_total_n`, `fy_total_n`, `mz_nm` | $\text{m/s}^2, \text{N}, \text{Nm}$ | Realized planar accelerations $(a_x, a_y)$ and net body forces/yaw moment |
| **Steering & Reference** | `steer_cmd_deg`, `steer_deg`, `desired_yaw_rate_rads`, `yaw_moment_cmd_nm` | $\text{deg}, \text{rad/s}, \text{Nm}$ | Driver commanded steer $\delta_{\text{cmd}}$, actuator state $\delta_{\text{act}}$, filtered reference $r_{\text{ref}}$, and outer controller $M_{z,\text{cmd}}$ |
| **Per-Wheel Kinematics & Forces** (`fl_`, `fr_`, `rl_`, `rr_`) | `<w>_omega_rads`, `<w>_kappa`, `<w>_alpha_rad`, `<w>_fx_n`, `<w>_fy_n`, `<w>_fz_n`, `<w>_peak_n`, `<w>_utilization` | $\text{rad/s}, 1, \text{rad}, \text{N}, 1$ | Wheel angular speed $\omega_i$, longitudinal slip $\kappa_i$, slip angle $\alpha_i$, wheel-frame forces $(F_{x,i}, F_{y,i}, F_{z,i})$, peak grip $D_i = \mu_{\text{eff}} F_{z,i}$, and Kamm utilization $\eta_i$ |
| **Per-Wheel Torques & Slip Control** | `<w>_drive_nm`, `<w>_brake_nm`, `<w>_drive_outer_nm`, `<w>_brake_outer_nm`, `<w>_slip_reduction`, `<w>_slip_active` | $\text{Nm}, 1, \{0,1\}$ | Effective inner-loop wheel torques vs. outer-loop allocated torques and ABS/ASR reduction factor $r_i$ |
| **Per-Wheel Friction** | `<w>_mu_true`, `<w>_mu_est` | $1$ | Ground-truth contact-patch road friction $\mu_{\text{road}}(X_i, Y_i)$ and perceived friction $\mu_{\text{est},i}$ |
| **Handling Indicators** | `understeer_index_rads`, `front_axle_utilisation`, `rear_axle_utilisation` | $\text{rad}, 1$ | Instantaneous self-steering index and RMS axle grip utilization |

## 2 Diagnostic Report Sections (`report.pdf` & `plots/*.png`)

The multi-page report generator (`src/torquevector/visualization/report.py`) produces ten specialized engineering views:

1. **`overview`**: Four-panel summary of trajectory $(X, Y)$, yaw-rate tracking $r(t)$ vs. $r_{\text{ref}}(t)$, sideslip angle $\beta(t)$, and corrective yaw moment $M_{z,\text{cmd}}(t)$.
2. **`kpi`**: Bar-chart comparison of controller yaw-rate RMSE, cross-track RMSE, peak sideslip $|\beta|_{\max}$, peak tyre utilization, and differential rear torque RMS.
3. **`tracking`**: Detailed tracking error residuals $e_r(t) = r(t) - r_{\text{ref}}(t)$ and cross-track deviation $e_y(t)$.
4. **`allocation`**: Outer-loop yaw moment command $M_{z,\text{cmd}}$ alongside individual wheel drive torques $T_{\text{FL}}, T_{\text{FR}}, T_{\text{RL}}, T_{\text{RR}}$ and rear left/right differential torque $\Delta T_{\text{rear}}$.
5. **`wheels_<controller>`**: Four-wheel breakdown of normal loads $F_{z,i}(t)$, longitudinal/lateral tyre forces $(F_{x,i}, F_{y,i})$, slip ratios $\kappa_i(t)$, and slip angles $\alpha_i(t)$.
6. **`friction_circle`**: Normalized polar Kamm-circle diagrams $(F_{y,i}/D_i,\; F_{x,i}/D_i)$ for `FL`, `FR`, `RL`, `RR` against the unit friction boundary $\sqrt{(F_x/D)^2 + (F_y/D)^2} = 1$.
7. **`energy`**: Mechanical power and cumulative work audit comparing kinetic energy change $\Delta E_{\text{kin}}$ against drivetrain input work and aerodynamic, rolling, and tyre-slip dissipation.
8. **`road_map` & `road_detail_<controller>`**: Spatial 2D heatmap of $\mu_{\text{road}}(X, Y)$ overlaid with the four wheel contact-patch trajectories, true vs. estimated friction $\mu_{\text{true},i}(t)$ vs. $\mu_{\text{est},i}(t)$, and wheel utilization.
9. **`slip_control_<controller>`**: High-rate ($200\text{ Hz}$) inner-loop diagnostic showing wheel slip $\kappa_i(t)$ vs. ABS/ASR thresholds, torque reduction factors $r_i(t)$, and outer vs. effective wheel torques.
10. **`handling` (`results/handling_iso4138/plots/`)**: ISO 4138 handling diagrams ($\Delta\delta$ vs. $a_y/g$), steady-state yaw-rate gain $r/\delta(v_x)$, $(\beta, r)$ phase-plane stability portraits, and quasi-static $g$-$g$ acceleration envelopes.
11. **`animations/comparison.gif` & `road_<controller>.gif`**: Synchronized 2D planar vehicle animations combining the full manoeuvre trajectory with oriented controller footprints, a true-scale 2D follow camera (steered front wheels, ghost `OFF` baseline, world-frame wheel force vectors, and CG velocity vector), a body-frame 2D $F_x / F_y / F_{\text{res}}$ diagram, live 4-wheel Kamm friction circles, and time-series tracks.
