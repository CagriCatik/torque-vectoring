# Scientific Review of Model Assumptions, Structural Boundaries, and Limitations

This document provides a critical scientific and engineering accounting of the modelling simplifications, numerical trade-offs, control-theoretic boundaries, and empirical validation limits of `torquevector`.

---

## 1 Epistemic and Experimental Validation Boundaries

1. **Absence of Physical Vehicle and Tyre Bench Measurements (`Blockers B1 & B5`, `Q10 = 0/10`)**:
   - All vehicle mass, inertia, and geometric parameters in [`configs/vehicles/generic_passenger_car.yaml`](../configs/vehicles/generic_passenger_car.yaml) represent synthetic engineering assumptions for a C/D-segment four-wheel-drive passenger vehicle.
   - The tyre dataset [`data/synthetic/synthetic_brush_measurements.csv`](../data/synthetic/synthetic_brush_measurements.csv) ($N = 2{,}520$) is generated by a synthetic two-dimensional isotropic brush-model truth generator (`src/torquevector/calibration/synthetic_generation.py`), not a flat-belt laboratory tyre test rig or mobile tyre test trailer.
   - Consequently, `Q02` ($7/10$), `Q03` ($9/10$, critical `Q03.2` $= 1/2$), and `Q10` ($0/10$, `REQUIRED_REAL_DATA`) remain intentionally blocked in the quality gate audit.

---

## 2 Chassis, Suspension, and Kinematic Simplifications

1. **Planar Rigid-Body Chassis (No Pitch, Roll, or Heave Degrees of Freedom)**:
   - The chassis models three planar degrees of freedom $(v_x, v_y, r)$ plus four wheel rotational states $(\omega_{\text{FL}}, \omega_{\text{FR}}, \omega_{\text{RL}}, \omega_{\text{RR}})$ and one first-order steering actuator state $\delta_{\text{act}}$.
   - Transient suspension vertical travel, pitch angle $\theta$, roll angle $\phi$, roll-centre heights, anti-dive/anti-squat geometry, and unsprung mass wheel-hop modes ($10\text{--}15\text{ Hz}$) are omitted.
2. **Fixed-Split Quasi-Static Lateral Load Transfer**:
   - Lateral load transfer $\Delta F_{z,y} = m a_y h_{\text{CG}} / t$ is partitioned between the front and rear axles strictly in proportion to the static longitudinal weight split ($b/L = 57.25\,\%$ front, $a/L = 42.75\,\%$ rear).
   - Dedicated front/rear roll stiffness distributions $k_{\phi,f} / (k_{\phi,f} + k_{\phi,r})$ via anti-roll bars and coil springs are not parameterized separately in the four-wheel force balance (axle-level handling variations are instead represented via `AxleTyreScaling` in `src/torquevector/physics/axle_tyre.py`).
3. **Parallel Front Steering and Zero Elasto-Kinematics**:
   - Front wheels steer in parallel ($\delta_{\text{FL}} = \delta_{\text{FR}} = \delta_{\text{act}}$) without Ackermann toe-out geometry, static toe, camber angle ($\gamma = 0$), camber thrust, or compliance steer under lateral/longitudinal forces.
   - Self-aligning torque $M_z^{\text{tyre}}$ (pneumatic trail $t_p(\alpha)$) is not added to the vehicle yaw moment balance.
4. **Fail-Closed Operating Envelope at Low Speed and Wheel Lift**:
   - Because longitudinal slip $\kappa_i = (R_{\text{eff}}\omega_i - u_{w,i})/u_{w,i}$ and slip angle $\alpha_i = \operatorname{atan2}(v_{w,i}, u_{w,i})$ become singular as $u_{w,i} \to 0$ without a transient tyre relaxation-length state ($\sigma_\kappa \dot{\kappa}_{\text{trans}} + u_w \kappa_{\text{trans}} = R_{\text{eff}}\omega - u_w$), the plant rejects wheel contact speeds $u_{w,i} < 3.0\text{ m/s}$, reverse driving ($v_x \le 0$), excess slip $|\kappa_i| > 0.5$, and wheel lift ($\min_i F_{z,i} \le 0$) via `OutsideEnvelopeError` (`FAIL_CLOSED`).

## 3 Tyre Mechanics and Combined-Slip Formulation

1. **Reduced 3-Parameter Magic Formula vs. Full Pacejka MF 5.2 / 6.12**:
   - Each pure-slip axis uses $(B, C, E)$ with load-sensitive peak factor $D(F_z) = \mu_{\text{road}} (F_z/F_{z,0})^{k_{\text{load}}} F_z$. Load dependence of shape and stiffness factors $B(F_z), E(F_z)$, horizontal/vertical shifts $S_H, S_V$ (ply-steer and conicity), and inflation-pressure/temperature scaling are not modelled.
2. **Opposing-Slip Lorentzian Damping Surrogate**:
   - Combined slip is represented by smooth opposing-slip damping $\tilde{F}_x = F_{x,0}/\sqrt{1 + (\alpha/\alpha_s)^2}$, $\tilde{F}_y = F_{y,0}/\sqrt{1 + (\kappa/\kappa_s)^2}$ followed by radial Kamm-circle projection, rather than empirical MF cosine weighting functions $G_{x\alpha}, G_{y\kappa}$ with $S_{Vy\kappa}$.
3. **No Thermal or Wear State**:
   - Tyre bulk/flash surface temperature, frictional heating power $\mathbf{F}_w \cdot \mathbf{v}_{\text{slip}}$, and tread wear are not simulated dynamically (`temperature: not_modelled`).

## 4 Control-Theoretic and Allocation Limitations

1. **Steady-State Yaw-Moment Droop in `LTV-MPC` and `NMPC` on Constant-Radius Circle (`Q11.3`)**:
   - While `LQR` ($+27.3\,\%$ to $+39.0\,\%$) and `P` ($+25.4\,\%$ to $+34.2\,\%$) exceed the $\ge 20\,\%$ yaw-rate RMSE reduction threshold across all four nominal Q11 manoeuvres, `LTV-MPC` ($+19.6\,\%$) and `NMPC` ($+18.9\,\%$) fall $0.4\text{--}1.1$ percentage points short on the $15\text{ m/s}$ steady-state circle (`circle_track`).
   - This arises from three deliberate configuration choices in `configs/controllers/{ltv_mpc,nmpc}.yaml`: (i) a conservative yaw-moment ceiling $M_{z,\max} = 900\text{ Nm}$ (vs. $2{,}000\text{ Nm}$ for `LQR`/`P`), (ii) stage cost regularization $w_u (M_z / M_{\text{scale}})^2$ ($w_u = 0.12$) on total yaw moment without an integral action or steady-state feedforward $M_{z,\text{ff}}$, and (iii) penalizing sideslip $(\beta - 0)^2$ during a constant-radius turn where kinematic steady-state sideslip $\beta_{\text{ss}} \neq 0$.
2. **Reduced-Order Single-Track Prediction vs. Full Four-Wheel NMPC**:
   - Both `LTV-MPC` and `NMPC` optimize a scalar corrective yaw moment $M_z$ over a two-state $(\beta, r)$ single-track prediction model rather than directly optimizing all four wheel torques $\mathbf{T} \in \mathbb{R}^4$ over the 11-state four-wheel combined-slip plant.
3. **Small-Angle Moment-Arm Approximation in BVLS Torque Allocation**:
   - The linear allocator approximates wheel yaw-moment arms by $\ell_i = -y_i / R_{\text{eff}}$ along the body $x$-axis, neglecting the geometric projection $\cos\delta_{\text{act}}$ and lateral offset $x_f \sin\delta_{\text{act}}$ of the steered front wheels.
4. **One-Way Coupling Between Outer Allocator and Inner Wheel-Slip Controller**:
   - The $5\text{ ms}$ inner-loop ABS/ASR controller (`WheelSlipController`) scales outer-loop wheel torques via $T_{\text{eff},i} = (1 - r_i) T_{\text{outer},i}$ without feeding the active torque reduction $r_i$ back to re-distribute the unfulfilled yaw moment $\Delta M_z$ onto the non-saturating wheels within the same $40\text{ ms}$ outer frame.
5. **Offline Particle Swarm Optimization (`torquevector optimize`)**:
   - PSO controller tuning (`src/torquevector/optimization/`) operates strictly offline over finite simulation horizons and is not a real-time adaptive controller.
